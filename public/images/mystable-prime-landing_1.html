Your Racing Edge

RaceVolt Core

Thirty engineered features per runner — form, pace, going, jockey and trainer strike rates — feed a gradient-boosted ensemble tuned for every race class.

Live Odds Feed

Real-time odds streamed from PlayaBets, 10Bet, LuckyFish, Betfred and Hollywoodbets, normalised into one comparable feed.

Calibrated Probabilities

Raw model scores are mapped to true win probabilities via isotonic regression — so a "30% chance" really does win three times in ten.

Kelly-Fraction Staking

A PPO reinforcement-learning agent chooses a Kelly-fraction action for every bet — sizing stakes to your edge, not your emotions.

Risk Alerts

Drawdown thresholds, bankroll caps and volatility alerts keep every session inside the limits you set.

Performance Analytics

Full history of every pick, stake and result — exportable reports that show exactly where the edge comes from.
